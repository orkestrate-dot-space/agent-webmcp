# How They're Doing — Traction & Status (Oct 2026)

## Pomerado (Akshay Manglik, Alan Ma — YC F26)
- **Stage:** Very early. Launched Product Hunt Oct 16, 2026 → 175 upvotes, #5 daily, 17 comments. GitHub core: 583★, 43 forks, 2,460 commits, MIT. Docs + REST API + MCP live. Pricing live ($0/$50/Business).
- **Funding:** YC F26 only disclosed (no separate round announced). 2-person team, SF.
- **Traction signals:** Demo video (206 views), Discord community, open-source core. No public customer logos or revenue disclosed. Benchmark claims are self-run.
- **Verdict:** Launch-phase. Strong narrative (7×/100×/30%), but unproven at scale; self-heal + auth brokerage unverified in production.

## Parse (Alex Forman — YC F25)
- **Stage:** Most traction of the trio. Launched Aug 2025, hit **30k users in 3 weeks**, 11k at a scaling crunch (temporarily limited access). Pre-seed closed Aug 2025. YC F25.
- **Product:** Reverse-engineers network requests → deterministic API, 10-100× faster/cheaper than browser automation. Added **MCP Mar 3, 2026**: "1000s of APIs already built," "500× faster than browser agents," book tables on Resy, search flights, etc. X: 8,116 followers.
- **Funding:** Pre-seed (Aug 2025, amount undisclosed). YC F25. Team 3-6.
- **Verdict:** Clear leader in raw user adoption among the three. MCP pivot (Mar 2026) aligns with the agent-tool wave. Risk: reverse-engineering network calls breaks on complex/SPA flows; reliability at scale was already a pain point.

## Rindler (Michael Serrano, Arthur De Los Santos — YC S26)
- **Stage:** Earliest. YC S26, 2-3 people, Boston (MIT '26 grads, Feb 2026). Launched "Rindler — Turn Any Website Into An API For AI Agents."
- **Product:** Hosted MCP, deterministic typed tools, logins/2FA/bot detection, agent-agnostic. **Self-described as "still a work in progress"** — "some sites map cleanly, others require more in-depth mappings, bot-defended or logged-in sites may need one-time credential/cookie capture."
- **Funding:** YC S26 only. No separate round disclosed.
- **Traction:** No public user/customer numbers. Looking for feedback from founders building products where reliable web actions are core.
- **Verdict:** Earliest-stage of the trio. Honest about limitations (work in progress, credential capture needed for defended sites). MIT founders, strong CS/AI background (Roblox ML, MIT CSAIL LLM research).

## Context.dev (Yahia Bakour — YC S26)
- **Stage:** Most funded/established of the adjacent set. **$9M seed** (bootstrapped → YC S26 → seed), 7 people, millions of requests/hour, 600+ companies (Mintlify, Klarna, Similarweb, Ferndesk, DocsBot, Vizzy, Comp AI, Squad).
- **Product:** Structured web data + brand/styleguide + search + monitors + MCP. More extraction than task execution, but the "context layer" play.
- **Verdict:** Strongest traction/funding in the adjacent set, but different category (web data, not minted task APIs). Growing fast.

## Others in the space
- **Firecrawl** (YC S22): $75M Series B, 1.5M+ users, Alexandria launch. The giant in extraction/interact.
- **Browser Use** (open source): 119k★ GitHub, cloud product, WebMCP directory (13,352 sites). Dominant browser-agent OSS.
- **Asteroid** (YC W25): Self-healing browser agents in healthcare/insurance/finance; Astro no-code builder launched; thousands of agents in production.
- **Reworkd** (YC S23): $4M raised; Playwright code-gen scraping at scale.
- **Kimono Labs** (historical): acquired by Palantir 2016, shut down — the cautionary tale for this category.

## Net read
- **Parse** is winning on raw adoption (30k users, MCP live) but is a solo founder scaling through crunches.
- **Pomerado** is the most ambitious product (self-heal + auth brokerage + agent-driven minting) but is the newest and least proven; its open-source core + benchmark narrative are the growth levers.
- **Rindler** is the earliest and most candid about gaps.
- **Context.dev** and **Firecrawl** are the funded incumbents in the broader "web data for agents" layer, not direct minted-API competitors.
- The whole category is 2025-2026 vintage; no one has yet proven self-healing minted APIs at enterprise scale. The 2026-2027 window (WebMCP adoption, browser-agent cost curves, self-heal claims being tested) will decide winners.
