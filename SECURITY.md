# Security

Report vulnerabilities to support@orkestrate.space. Include what you ran,
what you expected, and what happened; reports are kept confidential.

In scope:

- The `agent-webmcp` CLI (finding, index verification, `setup`).
- The `find` MCP server.
- The intake and index Workers (`intake/`, `worker/`) and the scan pipeline.

Out of scope: the indexed sites themselves. A tool that misbehaves belongs to
its site; a listing problem (wrong tools, a site that should not be listed)
is a data issue, not a vulnerability — email support@orkestrate.space and a
maintainer applies the takedown.

There is no bounty program.
